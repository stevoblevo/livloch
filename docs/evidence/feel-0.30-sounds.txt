integrated loudness; fine for comparing short sounds); spectral centroid Hz; share of energy in 2-5 kHz; clicks (1 ms
windows whose energy above 12 kHz is over 20 % of their total, within 30 dB of the loudest window); off% = share of
tonal energy (peaks 60 Hz-2 kHz) not on a degree of the key (glides and noise read as off; see notes); length s.
