0.29 play: what 0.28 left. What was built, what was tried and dropped, and what it measured.
Everything here is simulator output from tools/tune.js (a scripted player, not a person, not a bot match) unless it says
soak (bots only). A row of 48 seeds has a standard error of about 7 points of win rate, a difference of two rows about 10.
This session met that noise head on: the same lesson-4 policy on Cruel measured 48, 52, 54, 56, 56, 63, 65, 67, 67 and 71%
across rule variants that barely touched it. Single rows under 15 points apart are not findings. Tables: play-0.29-before.txt,
play-0.29-after.txt.

WHY EVEN WITH NO LESSONS ROSE IN 0.28 (measured; table at the end of play-0.29-before.txt)
  All 0.28 rules on 56%. Without the slack thread 54%. With it but no leaning hollow 58%. Without lights 48%. Without
  arrivals 42%. Without all three 38%, which is 0.27's number exactly. So: the slack thread was not it. The two bot
  teammates were. A bot rides out from home every time it has been back, lets go behind its own line, and in 0.28 that
  landed an arrival (140 and more to every small Hush for 24 m ahead) each time; and a bot that went home hurt came back
  with a light. They were playing lessons 4 and 5 for a player who knew none.
  The fix is a rule, not a number: an arrival now needs a landing among the Hush (a Hush trooper within 8 m of where the
  rider lets go). A person who rides into them gets it; a bot that lets go behind its own troopers lands quietly.

THE RULES (sim.js "0.29 play"; numbers in core.js)
  An arrival needs company   C.ARRIVE.among. Above.
  Three ways to a light      mended at home (0.28); or stand 3 s in the fountain, hurt or not; or catch a small one.
  A kept house               the house that takes a light is kept for 80 s (was: bright for 30 s). It fires twice as fast
                             (0.28), takes 35% of all harm, is never slack even on a slack thread, and no small one picks
                             it. Any standing house takes a light now, whole or hurt.
  The Hush patron lives on its houses   every Hush guardian or warden that falls, on any thread, takes 12% of the Hush
                             patron's full health with it. Long match only. Event patrondim. This is the rule that made
                             lesson 5 count: before it, harm that was spread over three threads did not win.
  The tides                  a thread goes slack at 1:40, then 90 s before each red rain and again as each rain ends
                             (1:40, 3:30, 6:00, 8:30, 11:00, 13:30), for up to 60 s, said 30 s ahead. The leaning hollow
                             sets out 28 s before, so it is seen coming and is at the house as it opens. 0.28 had 2:30,
                             7:30, 12:30 with the hollow leaving as the thread went slack: it reached the house with a few
                             seconds of the fifty left.
  A dark house's clock runs on a slack thread (it stood still there in 0.28; found by a test).
  New events: patrondim (u the patron, by the house, n, amt). ember gains waited and thief. No other new event kinds.

WHAT THE SCRIPTED PLAYER DOES NOW (tools/tune.js header)
  Lesson 5: stops running to guardian fires (0.28); STAYS on its thread when another goes slack (0.28 rode to the far
  house); carries a light to the house that needs it most, where lessons 0 to 4 leave it in the first house they walk
  past. On Cruel it places 4.7 lights a match against 1.7.
  Every lesson: walks home to a point beside the patron. Until 0.29 a rider coming home down the middle thread walked
  into its own patron and stood there until its health came back by itself. That bug is part of why 0.28 measured
  going home as a loss; it does not explain all of it (below).

WHAT WAS TRIED AND DID NOT WORK (48 seeds, Cruel, lesson 4 row then lesson 5 row; runs kept in the session only)
  Each was built, tested and measured before it was dropped; the code is in the branch history (06e0c00 to 1a65d35).
  - Riding home by choice for a light, with a lit house sending its waves out half again as strong for 60 s:   54 / 42.
  - Riding home for a light as a rain or a tide is about to come, to keep one house through it:                67 / 48,
    and with tides every 100 s 67 / 54. It keeps more Reign houses (4.0 lost against 4.5) and wins less.
  - A light carried to a Hush house blinds it for 25 s (cannot fire, unprotected, harm x1.5):                  71 / 56,
    and with the patron blindable and tides every 100 s 52 / 44. 5.8 houses blinded a match, more Hush houses down, fewer wins.
  - Lights from thieves spent on blinding instead of keeping:                                                    56 / 52.
  - Riding the slack thread to the far house (lesson 5 as 0.28 had it), under the final rules:                 63 / 56 on
    seeds 1-48 and 54 / 58 on seeds 49-96, against 77 and 73 for staying.
  - Tides every 100 s; tides that last 80 or 90 s; a third tide to each rain (not run: it would fell a house every 80 s).
  - Gentle's Hush buildings 1.65 for longer matches: 7:35 to 9:55, still under nine minutes with lessons 3 to 5. Not adopted.
  What these have in common: every errand that takes the hero off its thread cost more than it returned, whatever the
  errand paid. In every such run lesson 5 lost fewer houses and took more than lesson 4, played longer, and then lost
  at the fray (15:00). It was winning the wrong thing. Two changes turned that round: harm anywhere now reaches the Hush
  patron, and lesson 5 stopped travelling (lights come to it from the small ones it catches).
  So the honest reading of target 1: choosing which house to keep is now a winning decision. GOING HOME is not: it is
  possible, quick (about 14 s there and back on the threads) and readable, and it did not measure as worth the trip.

WHAT STILL LOOKS WEAK
  - The quiet stretches. More tides did not shorten the longest one: a tide that fells nothing (a kept house, a hollow
    killed on the way) leaves five minutes between falls, and about half of all matches have one.
  - Gentle is short: 7 to 9 minutes. The patron rule and the tides both shorten it.
  - Even's slope is 48 / 69 / 48 for lessons 0, 1, 2. Lesson 2 is not worth what lesson 1 is.
  - Cruel with three lessons is 54% (0.28: 40%), so "Cruel only with four" is softer: 15 / 54 / 63 / 77 for 2, 3, 4, 5.
  - Bots alone win more on Even (5 of 12 against 1 of 12). Twelve seeds; the direction is real, the size is not known.
  - Lesson 5's gain was found after many variants were looked at on seeds 1-48. Seeds 49-96 were run once, after the
    rules and the policy were fixed, and agree (+19). That second set is the evidence; the first set chose the rules.
    The second set ran one commit before the last two small changes (a dark house's clock on a slack thread; the patron
    rule kept out of First Rain, which cannot touch a long match).
  - Nothing of these rules is drawn or has a sound. They are said in banners and the feed (ui.js lines marked 0.29 play).
    No frames were captured: there is nothing new on screen but words.
