WHY THE QUIET WAS THERE (play-0.30-before.txt, the lines at the end)
  The first tide (1:40) fells the Reign's middle guardian at about 1:56 in nearly every match. The next tide (3:30)
  picks the most worn thread, the middle again, where the leaning hollow now faces a warden it cannot fell in sixty
  seconds and the Hush guardian is too far for the Reign's wave. Nothing falls until the first rain's hollows arrive at
  about 5:45. In 21 of the first 30 Even matches with no lessons the longest quiet stretch began at that first fall.

THE RULES (sim.js "0.30 play"; numbers in core.js)
  The tide takes a house    A slack thread that runs its sixty seconds with no house fallen pulls itself tight and takes
                            one: of the two front houses on it, one a side, the one that has lost the larger share of
                            its health falls. A kept house (one that took a light) is never taken; two houses equally
                            worn, or both whole, and nothing falls. Said ten seconds before with the house named. Even and
                            Cruel (C.DIFF slackTake); not Gentle, where the Hush does not lean. A house felled by a hero
                            or a hollow before then still pulls the thread tight at once, as before.
                            On Even and Cruel 4.3 to 5.1 of about 5.4 tides a match now end with a house fallen; the
                            tide itself takes 0.3 to 0.8 Reign houses and 1.0 to 1.5 Hush houses a match.
  Bots and the slack house  A bot teammate no longer singles out a slack house (C.SLACK.bot 0). With the take, two bots
                            shooting the open house were running the race for a player who knew nothing (Even, no
                            lessons, 58% with them at it, 54% without; one row each, so the reason is the principle,
                            not the four points).
  Gentle                    Hush buildings 1.35 -> 1.6; a slack house on Gentle takes each blow once, not twice
                            (C.DIFF slackMul 1); each fallen Hush house takes 6% of the Hush patron, not 12% (dimK 0.5).
                            First Rain keeps its own 1.2.
  New events: slacklast (lane, u the house that would go, t seconds left), slacktake (lane, u, team). slackend and
  structdown carry took: true when the tide took the house. No other new event kinds.

THE LESSON-2 DIP: POLICY OR RULE
  Measured on 0.29's rules, Even, seeds 1-48 (play-0.30-before.txt). Thief hunting and little fruit were not it (50, 48
  with each off, against 48). The three habits around a house were: all on 48, all off 69 (lesson 1 is 69). One left on
  at a time: standing under a slack Hush house 46, falling back to its own house in a full rain 54, pressing while the
  hollow is down 63. So it was the scripted policy, mostly two habits, each reading a rule too simply:
   - it went under the open Hush house the moment its thread went slack, and the leaning hollow walked past it to the
     player's own open house;
   - "its own house" in a rain was the warden once the guardian had fallen (which it has by 1:56), sixty metres back.
  Both are changed in tools/tune.js: under the open house only when no hollow is on its thread; back in a rain only to a
  guardian (letters D and G bring the old habits back). That, the take and the bot change moved lesson 2 from 48 to 60.
  What is left (60 against 68 over 96 seeds) no single habit accounts for: with thieves, pressing or fruit switched
  off one at a time under the final rules lesson 2 is 63, 58, 65, and with all three house habits off it was 63. Lesson 2
  loses fewer houses and takes more than lesson 1 (4.1-4.1 against 4.5-3.8) and still loses its patron more often: its
  errands are off the middle thread, the patron's, which has had no guardian since 1:56. That is a rule, not a policy:
  the first tide always costs the player's own thread its guardian, so every errand afterwards is paid for at the warden.
  Not changed here; the next thing to try is in WHAT STILL LOOKS WEAK.
