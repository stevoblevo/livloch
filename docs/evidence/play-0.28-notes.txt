0.28 play: lessons 4 and 5, and the dead stretches. What was built, why, and what it measured.
Everything here is simulator output from tools/tune.js (a scripted player, not a person, not a bot match).
A row of 48 seeds has a standard error of about 7 points of win rate; a difference between two rows about 10.
Differences under 15 points between two rows are not findings.

WHAT 0.27 LEFT (play-0.28-before.txt, 48 seeds)
  Cruel  lessons 123 44%, 1234 52%, 12345 38%.  Even none/1/12: 38/60/69%.
  Median longest stretch with no building falling: Cruel 4:14 to 4:42, Even 3:53 to 5:34.
  A tool written for this (what harmed each house, by source and by phase; not committed, the event field it reads is:
  'hit' events now carry src) showed three things on Cruel:
   1. Small Hush troopers, not hollows, do most of the harm to Reign houses (about 12,000 of 23,000 a match), in the
      calm between rains. A thread without a hero on it loses its guardian in about half a minute.
   2. Hush houses are brought down by heroes (25,000 of 28,000), spread over three threads. A win needs 23,000 on one
      thread and the patron, so harm that is spread does not win.
   3. The quiet stretches were the opening (nothing falls before the first rain's hollows arrive, about 5:45) and the
      wait between rains.

THE RULES (sim.js "0.28 play"; numbers in core.js)
  Arrival        a ride of 45 m toward the front lands as a blow and a stagger around the landing and 24 m ahead.
  The fold       the thread folded away is shut to the Hush for the 45 s: small ones crumple in the crease, a hollow
                 is pinned there and loses 4% of its health a second. Before, folding left that thread open and
                 measured at nothing.
  A light        the fountain mends 40% of a hero in one visit: it leaves with a light; on foot by a hurt or dark
                 house of its side the house is lit, mended 30%, and fires twice as fast for 30 s.
  The slack thread  at 2:30, 7:30, 12:30 (halfway between rains, said 20 s ahead) the most worn thread goes slack for
                 up to 50 s: houses of both sides on it stop firing, lose their protection and take double harm; on
                 Even and Cruel the Hush sends a hollow down it; the thread carries a rider to the far side's open
                 house. The first house to fall pulls it tight; if it was a Hush house the leaning hollow crumples.
  Gentle's Hush buildings 1.2 -> 1.35 (the fold and the slack thread had Gentle matches ending near 8:30); First Rain
  keeps 1.2. No other number in C.DIFF moved.

WHAT THE SCRIPTED PLAYER DOES NOW (tools/tune.js header)
  Lesson 4: rides, lands arrivals, folds the next thread into its own (kept for the rain when one is near).
  Lesson 5: stops running to guardian fires; rides the slack thread to the far house; walks a light to the house that
            needs it most.
  Dropped from lesson 5: walking home at 35% instead of 12%.

WHAT WAS TRIED AND DID NOT WORK (40 or 48 seeds on Cruel unless said; runs not kept, numbers from the session)
  - Going home early or by choice to fetch lights (lesson 5 as 0.27 had it, and a version that went only when its
    thread was quiet), with the light worth 20%, 35% and 50% of a house: 35 to 55%, never above lesson 4. Time off
    the thread costs more than a mended house returns. The light is real and readable but it is not a reason to go
    home; it pays whoever had to go anyway.
  - A slack thread on a random lane: lesson 4 and 5 equal to lesson 3 (43/35 to 43% against 45%). Harm was spread.
    Choosing the most worn thread is what made the slack thread count.
  - A slack thread without the Hush's hollow: nothing falls on it, the quiet stays near 5:00.
  - Slack harm 1.5 instead of 2, and no race (the thread stays slack 50 s whoever falls): lesson 5 48% against 60%.
  - Lesson 5 folding its own thread into the slack one before it leaves (F): 54 to 65%, no better.
  - Lesson 5 giving up the slack house when its own is in danger (R): 52%.
  - Keeping the crease for the rain (lesson 4): 73% with, 71% without. Kept in the policy; not a finding.
  - Arrivals are rare in the scripted player (0.1 to 0.7 a match): it rides only when coming back from home.
    Their worth is unmeasured.

WHAT STILL LOOKS WEAK
  - Lesson 5 is not worth a win on Cruel. The honest reading is that in this model a hero's time on a thread is
    worth more than anything it can choose to do elsewhere, and nothing built here changed that enough.
  - Even is easier than 0.27 for a player who knows nothing (56%).
  - Nothing of these rules is drawn in the world or has a sound: they are said in banners and the feed only.
  - tools/test.js: the eight bot-only matches with the Ness now end with two of eight seeing no chomp tell (29 tells
    over the eight, was 36); the per-match check for a tell became a total. tools/soak.js (bot-only win rates)
    was not rerun: expect it to have moved.
